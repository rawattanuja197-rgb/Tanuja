🌱 What is Sustainable Energy?

Sustainable energy is energy produced from sources that can naturally replenish themselves and can be used for a long time without seriously harming the environment.

Unlike fossil fuels such as coal, oil, and natural gas, sustainable energy generally produces much lower greenhouse-gas emissions and helps reduce pollution.

Solar Energy

Solar energy comes from the sun and can be converted into electricity or heat using solar panels and other technologies.

Wind Power

Wind energy uses the movement of air to generate electricity through wind turbines.

Hydropower

Hydropower generates electricity using the movement of water.
